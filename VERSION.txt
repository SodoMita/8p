EIGHT PAGES 1.6.5
Godot 4.7.2 · GL Compatibility · GDScript

1.6.5 outside forest and real tree collisions (#49). Trees beyond the fence now
     use the same six leafless meshes, bark material, scale/lean range and buried
     roots as the arena forest. Both forests collide against reusable concave mesh
     shapes made from the visible lower-trunk triangles (the player contact band),
     rather than cylinders or collision-free MultiMeshes. Chunk streaming removes
     those colliders with their trees; phone chunk-build budget is reduced to keep
     physics registration smooth. The suite is 79 world + 30 rig + 27 control checks.
1.6.4 stump cut faces (#70). Stumps now use separate bark-side and end-grain cap
     surfaces. The visible cut face gets its own UV-mapped PBR maps with irregular
     growth rings, a pith knot, narrow radial splits and fine relief; the forest
     batch preserves the two surface materials. Added geometry/material checks,
     a Sway-rendered stump preview, and deterministic texture generation.
     The suite is 75 world + 30 rig + 27 control checks.
1.6.3 physical page pins (#55). A page is no longer pinned by a guessed landmark
     coordinate. Each nail point is resolved by a real physics ray and matched to
     a rendered triangle on the same collider; a second short ray verifies the
     visible/collision contact and the player has room to stand in front. One
     visible rigid-body nail passes through the cloth at the verified point. E
     pulls the nail and releases the sheet into gravity/flutter; a second E takes
     it. No surface found means no fake pin. The weathered rocks now use their
     rendered mesh as the static collider so nails cannot hover over a cylinder
     approximation. Selftest also frees its probe map before physics checks (the
     queued duplicate colliders had been causing false blocked-page reports).
     The suite is 71 world + 30 rig + 27 control checks.
1.6.2 pointer owner + input rule (human #53/#54). The pointer is captured only
     while the run is live and no screen needs clicking: world._sync_pointer() is
     the only owner, hud.gd writes Game.ui_blocking when a pause or end screen
     opens or closes, and player.gd refuses to capture while one is open and drops
     a captured pointer the frame one appears -- the death screen used to keep the
     cursor captured (it was only released on touch devices) so its buttons were
     unreachable. The look path has no platform or device branches left: pointer
     motion turns the camera unless a finger is down or the pointer is over a
     widget. The camera also stopped dying on a 4x4 crosshair dot whose ColorRect
     kept Control's default STOP filter at screen centre, exactly where a captured
     pointer lives. New tools/mouse_live_test.sh drives a real X11 pointer and
     asserts the camera turns; F3 in game shows the live input path.
1.6.0 branch integration. Six agent branches merged into main and made to run
     together. What actually needed fixing to make them coexist:
     * translations (RU/ZH): the branch's manager could not load at all -- it
       declared get_instance() returning a class it never named, called the
       Godot 3 find_node(), and used get_tree() from a static function. Both
       language files were also invalid JSON (a literal backslash-n before every
       real newline) and translations.json was markdown. Rewritten as a real
       singleton with an English fallback table, a Language enum, and the choice
       persisted in settings.cfg. en.json is generated from the diff that
       replaced the literals, so fallbacks are the original wording.
     * labyrinth: it returned early from _build_landmark and appended its own
       page spot at the maze centre with no normal and no height -- a ninth page
       lying on the sanctum floor and no page on a wall. It now leaves a real
       anchor (a standing corridor wall, face turned into the cell) and goes
       through the same page path as every other landmark. Its lab_* materials
       use the triplanar stone path, and the orphaned appear.wav the OGG
       migration left behind is gone.
     * bathroom page: it hung on the back wall, which the stall dividers seal
       off (they span w-0.7, wider than either side gap). Moved to the side wall
       in the open floor between the stalls and the door.
     * truck: the model pass's detailed wheels and hub merged with our pinned
       page anchor on the driver's door.
     * concrete: the rotation-following metre projection now also drives the
       concrete material (it blends cracks into the real stone maps instead of
       faking them with noise). ShaderMaterial has no
       vertex_color_use_as_albedo, so the shader multiplies COLOR itself to keep
       the labyrinth's batched tints.
     * bark keeps the 1.5.2 fix (lifted albedo, softened relief, wrapped
       diffuse) on top of the new world-anchored projection.
     Also merged: 3D model pass (detailed trees, snags, pumpkins, truck, batched
     prop details, weathered metal), mouse look decided by event origin, and the
     restored grain/vignette.
     Not merged: improve/slender-model. It is a competing Slender (procedural
     anatomy, 1.13 m hips, one mesh per limb) against the Anny/MakeHuman body
     already on main (5 batched surfaces, 2.26 m hips, IK deformation through
     slender_motion.gdshader). Its animation is the same algorithm already
     retargeted to anny; merging it would replace the reviewed asset work.
     71 world + 30 rig + 27 control checks.
     Audio follows the labyrinth branch's second pass (cardiac heartbeat, granular
     leaf footsteps, loop-seam and DC fixes) plus tools/audio_audit.py, which
     exits non-zero if any cue is noisy, offset, clipped or clicks on wrap.
1.5.2 fence orientation + bark at glancing light.
     * fence panels and gate leaves were rotated 90 degrees: the yaw put the
       plane's WIDTH along the outward normal, so every bay stood sideways to the
       run it belongs to. Panels, rails and their colliders now share one explicit
       basis (`_along_basis`) whose local X lies along the fence line, so all four
       runs and both gate leaves line up. Guarded by a check that every panel's
       local X is perpendicular to its side's outward normal.
     * bark no longer falls to black at steep angles. The albedo was dark to start
       with (0.20 linear) and the normal map steep (18 deg mean tilt), so a
       glancing torch left only specular crumbs. The material now lifts the albedo,
       softens the relief and uses wrapped diffuse so the terminator does not clip
       to zero. Same for the shed's planks.
1.5.1 pages: pinned to real surfaces, dark, and carrying their message.
     * every page is now attached to the thing it belongs to - the face of the
       broken wall, the inside of the tunnel, the silo shell, the truck's side,
       the shed's back wall, a standing stone, the back of the bathroom, and a
       scrap board nailed across the tower's legs - instead of floating in a
       clearing. Each landmark reports a surface point plus an outward normal.
     * no light of their own at all: the per-page OmniLight went earlier, now
       the emission is gone too. Measured against the wall beside it, a page is
       0.81x as bright with the torch on and 0.86x in the IR mode - it is lit,
       not lit-up.
     * the note is on the page. The eight messages are baked into
       assets/textures/note_N.png by tools/gen_notes.py (runtime SubViewport
       capture was tried and is unreliable with eight live viewports), and the
       cloth sheet uses that image as its albedo, so what you read in the world
       is what you collect.
     * the sheet hangs off its nails: top edge pinned, wall-side clamped to the
       surface, calm flutter instead of a flag, two nail heads drawn in.
     * the inverted-face bug is gone and is now guarded: winding and normals
       both point out of the wall, asserted per page in world space.
     * map_builder: page spots carry a normal; a wall page used to be invisible
       from the front because the visible face was a back face.

1.5.4-labyrinth  audio: footsteps rebuilt as granular fracture, not resonators.
     * the previous step sounds were a handful of damped sine modes with long
       decays. Physically that is a woodblock, and that is what it sounded like
       (tonal 0.85, centroid 150-300 Hz: a plastic brick). Dry leaves, grit and
       soil are not resonators - they are thousands of sub-millisecond broadband
       fractures.
     * forest steps are now a decaying-Poisson grain cloud (0.9-6.2 kHz, ~300
       grains in the first 50 ms plus a sparse settling tail to 180 ms) over a
       broadband soil thud, a low-mid 'ground giving way' layer and a separate
       toe roll. Stone steps are the same idea at a harder setting: one
       sub-millisecond snap, a slab thud, a forefoot slap and sparse bright
       mortar grit.
     * measured: tonal 0.85 -> 0.09-0.12, centroid 150-300 Hz -> 1.9-2.4 kHz,
       graininess +3x, SFM 0.01 (a few pure tones) -> 0.19. audio_audit.py now
       enforces a tonal ceiling and a graininess floor on every footstep.

1.5.3-labyrinth  audio: a real heart, and two audit rounds over every stream.
     * heartbeat rebuilt from cardiac physiology (tools/gen_sfx.py): S1 = AV
       valve closure, S2 = semilunar closure, each a bank of damped leaflet and
       blood-column modes excited by a pressure step plus a turbulent jet
       pushed through those same modes, conducted through a chest wall that
       resonates at 68 Hz. S1->S2 is 319 ms at rest and 244 ms running; RR
       intervals wander +-3.9% (respiratory sinus arrhythmia); S3 on long
       beats, S4 before S1 when the heart is working. Rest loop 6.8 s at 68 bpm,
       strained loop 4.0 s at 125 bpm; they crossfade on dread or an empty
       stamina bar (Sfx.set_heartbeat).
     * every stream is DC-blocked at 18 Hz in write_ogg (footsteps had 0.009-
       0.011 of offset: headroom spent on a constant plus a thump at both ends);
       loops are closed with close_loop, whose tail-into-head crossfade is cut
       away so the wrap is sample-continuous (wind 0.30 -> 0.07, wire hum
       12.4 -> 0.38); everything is held under -1 dBFS before encoding
       (jumpscare was touching 0 dBFS).
     * tools/audio_audit.py: objective gate for all 25 streams (SFM, tonal,
       centroid, crest, kurtosis, envelope CV, DC, loop seam, loudness range)
       plus a cardiac physiology report. 25 streams, 0 flagged.
     * merged origin/main 1.5.2: camcorder pass, physics handheld rig, pinned
       cloth pages, 3.68 m Anny Slender, triplanar 2048 px concrete, endless
       exterior terrain and the control fixes all live alongside the labyrinth.
       64 selftest + 30 rig + 20 control checks green.
