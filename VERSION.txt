EIGHT PAGES 1.5.1
Godot 4.7.2 · GL Compatibility · GDScript
----------------------------------------
1.5.1 control audit: five input bugs fixed and pinned by a new 20-check harness
     (--autotest=controls). A finger produced look twice (touch overlay plus the
     emulated mouse motion from pointing/emulate_mouse_from_touch) so the touch
     camera ran ~1.7x too fast; a free cursor in touch mode turned the camera on
     every move; the touch overlay kept steering and could latch a stick while
     paused or dead; add_look() ignored frozen/paused; and the key that dismisses
     a note also fired flashlight/interact/pause. Pause now silences the overlay,
     the death freeze holds the view still, a real click re-captures the pointer
     on any device, and the cursor free-vs-captured rule is explicit. 34 selftests
     + 30 rig + 20 control checks green.

1.5  the map is closed and the world outside it keeps going.
     * boundary: a real chain-link fence, built the way one is built — 3 m
       mesh bays, galvanised posts, top rail, bottom tension wire, and a
       two-leaf gate standing open at the east side. 325 colliders on the
       ring, checked arithmetically (ring closure, per-run coverage, gate
       clearance) instead of by walking a character into it.
     * outside: endless procedural forest streamed in 32 m chunks around the
       player, plus a crowned dirt road that leaves through the gate and
       wanders to the horizon. A 4 km floor slab means there is no edge to
       fall off even if you go over the wire.
     * pages are verlet cloth now: 7x9 pinned grid, fixed 60 Hz, shear links,
       smoothed live normals, light coming through the sheet — and they carry
       no light of their own.
     * concrete/stone moved to world-space triplanar at 1.2 m per repeat:
       2048² maps at ~1700 px/m on every face, no stretch (was up to 46:1),
       isotropically rebuilt maps (runoff streaks and the per-tile moss band
       removed, aggregate-led tone), mipmapped.
     * tree normals re-verified the hard way: every triangle is wound with
       its vertex normals, and every sampled face passes a step-out-and-shoot
       -back test; the black caps at trunk bases were the downward-facing base
       fans showing above ground, so trees now sit 12 cm into the soil.
     * two deepseek audit agents ran in parallel during this cycle; all eleven
       of their cloth/terrain findings are fixed. 38 selftest checks.

     rolling band, tape tear/dropout, signal-scaled grain, night-lift grade, IR
     night-vision tube) and a viewfinder scene with REC/timecode/battery; the

     and the rig. 32 selftests + 27 rig checks green.

     sting is a real scream now (harmonic stack + 5 formants + pitch break +
     torn breath) layered over a subharmonic growl, a metal shriek and a sub
     impact, in stereo with room; three apparition takes, a close whisper, a
     chase roar, strained breathing, a minor-second drone, irregular heartbeat;
     Master-bus limiter. 29 selftest checks still green.
